## Laser Cut Parts

Laser cutting services require a separate file for each part. I have taken the original DXF file and split them into into individual files.

### 6mm Parts

* S275 Steel

### 3mm Parts

* CR4 Steel (supplier advised that S275 isn't typically available in thicknesses under 3mm and that CR4 is the standard grade mild steel for that thickness)

## Finishing

I specified "Level 2 - Fully Finished: Parts with consistent surface finish suitable for use as is or for coating/further finishing" because I also wanted the parts powder coated. This removed all dross, fully deburred and edges broken. Conisistent finish on all surfaces.

## Powder Coating

My parts were all powder coated with a dark grey satin finish (Anthracite - RAL 7016).
