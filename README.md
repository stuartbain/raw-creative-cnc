### Power Supplies

Mean Well branded power supplies are recommended for their "good, reliable" power supplies. They have trim pots to adjust the voltages accurately when using a multimeter. Be cautious on AliExpress as there are a number of cheaper Mean Well clones with similar branding and model names which may not have the same quality and reliability as the originals.

#### 48V

1 x [LRS-600-48 MEANWELL 600W 48VDC 12.5A 115/230VAC Enclosed Switching Power Supply](https://www.stepperonline.co.uk/lrs-600-48-meanwell-600w-48vdc-12-5a-115-230vac-enclosed-switching-power-supply-lrs-600-48.html)

- Used to power the 4 x 4A stepper motors
 - Adjusted down from 48V to 45V using trim pot and checked with a multimeter. The DM556T stepper drivers support a maximum 50VDC including back EMF generated by the stepper's motion. We provide a 10% overhead to reduce potential damage to the stepper drivers (50V - 10% (5V) = 45V) - Source: [PrintNC Wiki](https://wiki.printnc.info/en/v3/wiring-details#why-adjust-the-48v-power-supply-to-45v)
 - Rule of Thumb for power supply wattage is 2/3 of motor current rating. So 4 x 4A x 45V * 2/3 = 480W - Source: [PrintNC Wiki](https://wiki.printnc.info/en/electronics/steppers#q-is-a-600w-48v-meanwell-psu-turned-down-overkill-for-4-x-42-amp-stepper-motors)

#### 12V

I used a 24V to 12V step down voltage regulator to power the 2 x [12V 80mm Noctua Fans](https://www.noctua.at/en/products/nf-r8-redux-1800-pwm) used for cooling.

1 x [Voltage Regulator DC 8V ~ 40V to 12V 3A 36W Converter](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DHQNRVMN)

 - This is overkill for powering 2 x 60mA fans but it is smaller than a separate 12V power supply and runs cool and efficiently.
